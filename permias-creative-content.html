Role Overview

Turning event themes into a full promotional campaign

PERMIAS Seattle is a student organization, and as Marketing & Creative Content Specialist, I was responsible for the visual side of how events got in front of students — from concept to the graphics people actually saw on their feed.

What I did

I created promotional campaigns and digital content to build awareness for student events and initiatives, expanding our audience reach through strategic content, graphic design, and social media marketing. I worked closely with event teams to align messaging and content timing with what was actually happening on the ground, which helped strengthen participation and community outreach.

Approach

How the campaign came together

Build the visual identity first

Established the Y2K look — colors, textures, and typography — before producing individual posts, so every piece of content felt part of one campaign.

Design in Photoshop

Used layered compositing, gradient overlays, and text effects in Photoshop to hit the early-2000s aesthetic the theme called for.

Coordinate with the event team

Timed content drops around event milestones and stayed in sync with the organizing team so messaging matched what was actually happening on the ground.
